2. Portal Features & Responsibilities

🏠 Resident Portal
Visitor pass generation, child safety departure permissions & SOS alert center, helper passcodes, bill payments (maintenance & utilities), digital document locker.
🏛️ Secretary Portal
Resident roster & unit occupancy allocation, official invitation links, committee voting agenda, financial dues collection, guard shift schedules, broadcast announcements.
🛡️ Security Guard
Main gate terminal pass code validator, QR scanner, walk-in visitor entry logging, child safety exit authorization checkpoint, emergency lockdown trigger.
👥 Committee Portal
Society resolution governance, capital expenditure approvals, annual general meeting minutes, vendor contract sign-offs, financial balance health.
🔧 Facility Portal
Maintenance ticket dispatch, plumbing and electrical work orders, elevator AMC tracker, clubhouse amenity reservations, diesel generator fuel logs.
📦 Vendor Portal
Service staff daily gate access passes, contracted service job cards, invoice submission, compliance certifications.
⚡ Platform Super Admin
Multi-society database provisioning, system audit logs, health and readiness telemetry, cross-tenant role administration.

🚀 How to Access & Test

  • Instant Mobile / Web Testing: Open https://safegate-chi.vercel.app/login. Tap the "✨ Demo Role Accounts (Tap to test)" button right below the form. Tap "Fill" to auto-enter credentials or "Log In" to jump directly into that role's dashboard.
  • On Android (APK): Install Dwellora.apk (11.4 MB). The updated build is connected to the live production server and includes client-side offline fallback so mobile testing never fails.
